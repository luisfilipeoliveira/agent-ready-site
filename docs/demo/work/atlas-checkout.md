# Atlas checkout

Source: https://luisfilipeoliveira.github.io/agent-ready-site/demo/work/atlas-checkout

> A one-page checkout for a mid-size online shop.

## Problem

Four steps and a forced sign-up lost a third of buyers between cart and payment.

## What we did

One page, guest checkout first, address lookup and saved cards.

## Outcome

Completed orders rose 18% in the first quarter.
