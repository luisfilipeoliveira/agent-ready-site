## Problem

Riders on a badly set-up bike get sore knees and backs, and stop riding after a few weeks.

## What we do

We measure the rider, adjust saddle, bars and cleats, and check the result on a short ride.

## Outcome

Nine in ten riders we fit come back for the yearly check.
